Workflow automation

Small automations, hours back every week

These are the kinds of processes I usually automate first. Each one is a few weeks of work, and the payoff shows up right away.

Material purchase requests

Today

Site engineer sends a WhatsApp photo, someone re-types it into Excel, approvals get lost in chats.

Automated

Mobile form → engineer → PM → finance (above a set limit). PO is generated and emailed to the vendor on approval.

Daily progress reports

Today

Calling each site at 7pm to ask what got done and how many workers turned up.

Automated

Each site submits a 2-minute report; missing reports get a reminder, and directors get one summary email.

Client & vendor follow-ups

Today

Overdue invoices tracked in a spreadsheet that only one person understands.

Automated

Automatic reminders at 7 / 15 / 30 days overdue, with escalation and a live receivables view.

Website enquiries

Today

Enquiry emails sit in a shared inbox until someone notices.

Automated

Every enquiry is logged as a lead, assigned to sales, and pinged on WhatsApp within seconds.

Something working in week one, not month three
